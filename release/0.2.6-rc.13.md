# Vista Turbo RS232 0.2.6 RC13

RC13 consolidates the corrected VISTA alarm taxonomy and records the accepted Home Assistant-native product architecture on top of the RC12 synchronization and command-model foundation.

## Alarm semantics

- Classify `B1/B2` exclusively as generic auxiliary alarm/restore. Auxiliary is not inferred to mean medical.
- Classify the documented burglary families independently: `41/42` perimeter, `51/52` interior, `61/62` 24-hour zone/CID 133 burglary, `71/72` day/night, and `81/82` entry/exit.
- Represent `31/32` as the distinct canonical `panic_audible` alarm class rather than burglary or auxiliary.
- Track simultaneous audible-panic evidence independently so one restore cannot clear another active alarm token.
- Preserve fail-safe alarm behavior across restore, disarm, reconnect, stale state, and incomplete authoritative snapshots.
- Do not infer carbon-monoxide classification from zone descriptors or from the live `nq` event alone; CO remains deferred until authoritative zone metadata exists.

## Consolidated foundation

- Preserve protocol-correct P0 synchronization: routine reconciliation polls arming status only, while Zone Status remains startup/recovery-only and valid `nq` events maintain zone state between snapshots.
- Preserve corrupt-frame freshness invalidation, debounced full recovery, deferred recovery during startup/programming, and reconnect-on-failed-recovery behavior.
- Preserve the canonical `VistaCommand` parser/compiler/planner, serialized transaction ownership, semantic MQTT execution, bounded local audit, and verification semantics introduced in RC12.

## Architecture

- Accept ADR 0001: Vista Turbo HASS is a Home Assistant-native alarm-system suite, not an MQTT-centric bridge architecture.
- Keep the Home Assistant App as the sole VISTA protocol/domain engine.
- Establish `custom_components/vistaturbo` as the future primary Home Assistant-facing integration and Supervisor ingress as the future authenticated management console.
- Keep MQTT supported during migration, but treat it as compatibility infrastructure rather than the architectural center for new functionality.
- Record explicit identity, credential, fail-safe, entity-model, API, quality, roadmap, and PR-review invariants for future work.

The architecture decision is directional documentation only in RC13; the native integration and ingress management application are not yet implemented.

The bridge is bumped to `0.2.6-rc.13`. The keypad card remains `0.3.25` because this release does not change frontend behavior.
